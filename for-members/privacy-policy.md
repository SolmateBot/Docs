# Privacy Policy

## Privacy Policy

**Solmate Discord Bot and Website**

Last updated: November 20, 2026

### 1. Who We Are

Solmate ("the Bot") is a Discord bot with Solana wallet tracking features, and this policy also covers the Solmate website ("the Website"). Together we call them "the Service." The Service is operated by Pieter Spruijt Development, based in the Netherlands (KvK number: \[add KvK number]) ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

Contact: privacy@sprtdev.com

### 2. What We Never Collect

* We **never** ask for, receive, or store private keys, seed phrases, or wallet passwords.
* The Service never asks you to connect a wallet or sign a transaction.
* We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising. Payments are handled by Stripe, so we never see or store your full card details.

If anyone claiming to be Solmate asks for your seed phrase, it is a scam.

### 3. Information We Collect

#### 3.1 Information from Discord

When you use the Bot, we receive from Discord:

* your Discord user ID and username;
* the ID of the server (guild) and channel where you use the Bot;
* the commands and interactions you send to the Bot.

We do not read or store the content of messages that are not commands or interactions directed at the Bot.

#### 3.2 Wallet Tracking Data

To provide wallet tracking, we store:

* the public Solana wallet addresses you ask the Bot to track;
* any labels, alert settings, and notification channels you configure;
* the Discord user, server, and channel these settings are linked to.

Wallet addresses are public on the Solana blockchain. However, once a wallet address is linked to your Discord account in our database, we treat it as personal data.

#### 3.3 Website Analytics

The Website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics may collect your approximate location, device and browser type, pages viewed, and a cookie identifier. Analytics cookies are only set if you consent where the law requires it, and you can withdraw consent at any time through the cookie banner or your browser settings.

#### 3.4 Technical and Log Data

Our server may keep technical logs (such as error logs and request logs including IP addresses) to keep the Service secure and working.

#### 3.5 Messages You Send Us

If you contact us by email or Discord, we keep that correspondence so we can respond to you.

#### 3.6 Website Login with Discord

When you log in to the Website with Discord, Discord shares your Discord user ID, username, and avatar with us. We do not request your email address or any other Discord permissions. We use this information only to recognize you, show your account and tracked wallets, and link your Website account to your Bot settings. A session cookie keeps you logged in; it is strictly necessary for the login to work. You can revoke Solmate's access at any time in Discord under User Settings, Authorized Apps.

#### 3.7 Payments

Paid features are processed by Stripe. When you pay, Stripe collects your payment details (such as card details, billing name, and any email address or billing address you enter at checkout) under its own privacy policy and acts as an independent controller for that data. We never see or store your full card details. We receive from Stripe your purchase or subscription status, plan, amounts, dates, and Stripe customer ID, and link them to your Discord user ID so we can unlock paid features. We keep payment and invoice records for as long as Dutch tax and accounting law requires.

### 5. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell your data. We share it only as follows:

* **Hosting.** The Service runs on a virtual private server (VPS) located in the Netherlands, operated and administered solely by us. No other staff or contractors have access to the data.
* **Helius (Solana RPC provider).** To monitor wallets, the Bot sends public wallet addresses and related blockchain queries to Helius. Helius is a third party with its own privacy policy. We do not send your Discord identity to Helius.
* **Discord.** The Bot operates on Discord and receives and sends data through Discord's platform. Discord acts independently under its own Privacy Policy and Terms.
* **Google Analytics.** Website usage data is processed by Google as described in Section 3.3.
* **Legal reasons.** We may disclose data if required by law or valid legal process, or if necessary to protect our rights, safety, or property, or those of our users or the public.
* **Stripe (payments).** If you buy paid features, Stripe processes your payment and acts as an independent controller of your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your purchase and subscription status, not your full card details.

### 6. International Transfers

Our server is in the Netherlands (European Economic Area). Some of the services we use, including Discord, Helius, Google, and Stripe, may process data outside the EEA, including in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (where the provider is certified) or Standard Contractual Clauses.

### 8. Your Rights

If you are in the EEA, UK, or another region with similar laws, you have the right to:

* access the personal data we hold about you;
* correct inaccurate data;
* delete your data;
* restrict or object to certain processing;
* receive your data in a portable format;
* withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

To exercise these rights, email privacy@sprtdev.com from or mentioning your Discord user ID so we can verify it is you. We aim to respond within one month. You can also remove your tracked wallets yourself through the Bot's commands.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl) or your local supervisory authority.

### 9. Security

We protect data with measures such as restricted server access, up-to-date software, and limiting what we store. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your rights, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

### 10. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age Discord requires in your country. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

### 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for material changes, announce them on our Discord server or the Website. Continued use after an update means you accept the updated policy, but where the law requires your consent, we will ask for it.
